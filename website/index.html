What it is

A living province

Holds, strongholds and regions each keep their own officials, notice boards and property registry. Ownership and rank mean something.

Make a living

Gathering, crafting, trade and bounty work. Skills advance with time spent, and going hungry slows you down.

Play together

Parties, companions, shared dungeons and an in-world chat and emote system — on vanilla Skyrim Special Edition plus SKSE.

How to get in
