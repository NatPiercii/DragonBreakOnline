DragonBreak Online · Server rules

Blood and Law

Nineteen rules that govern play on DragonBreak. They are numbered so they can be cited: staff and players refer to them by number.

IC
In character. Your character's own knowledge and actions.
OOC
Out of character. You, the player, speaking as yourself.
NLR
New Life Rule. What your character forgets on death — rule 9.
Rules 1–4

Playing in character

  1. 1

    Remain In Character During Active RP

    When involved in an active RP scene, remain in character. OOC communication should be kept to designated methods unless necessary to resolve a technical issue or misunderstanding.

  2. 2

    No Metagaming

    You may only act on information your character has learned in-game. Information obtained through Discord, streams, OOC conversations, or another character you play cannot be used IC.

  3. 3

    No Powergaming

    Do not use game mechanics to force unreasonable outcomes or gain advantages that would not make sense within RP. Skyrim's mechanics exist to support roleplay, not replace it.

  4. 4

    No Exploiting Game Mechanics

    Do not abuse bugs, glitches, terrain, loading zones, AI behavior, animations, or other game mechanics to gain an unfair advantage during RP or combat. If a mechanic clearly produces an unintended advantage that your character would not reasonably have, do not use it.

Rules 5–6

Before blades are drawn

  1. 5

    Roleplay Before Combat

    PvP must have a reasonable IC cause and should generally involve appropriate escalation before combat begins. Randomly attacking or killing another player without sufficient RP justification is prohibited.

    Immediate violence may be appropriate when the circumstances reasonably justify it, such as an ambush, assassination, ongoing battle, or immediate defense of yourself or another person.

  2. 6

    Value Your Life

    Characters should react reasonably to situations where their life is in serious danger. Being heavily outnumbered, surrounded, captured, or threatened with deadly force should influence your character's actions.

    This does not mean every character must surrender. Reckless, courageous, fanatical, or stubborn characters are allowed, but their behavior should remain believable.

Rules 7–9

Death and what follows

  1. 7

    Defeat & Character Death

    Being defeated in combat does not mean your character is permanently dead. Permanent character death is determined by the player unless otherwise established through an approved server event or system.

  2. 8

    Do Not Immediately Return After Death

    If your character dies during an active conflict, you may not respawn and immediately return to that conflict. Your death triggers the server's New Life Rule.

  3. 9

    New Life Rule (NLR)

    Upon death, your character forgets the 30 minutes immediately preceding their death.

    30minutes forgotten

    Information, events, identities, conversations, and circumstances learned during those 30 minutes may no longer be used by your character.

    You may not use OOC knowledge to reconstruct what happened or immediately return to the location of your death to continue the same conflict.

Rules 10–13

Mercy, capture and crime

  1. 10

    Yielding & Surrender

    Players should be given a reasonable opportunity to surrender when circumstances allow. Once a character clearly yields, their surrender should be respected as part of the RP.

    Surrender does not protect a character from reasonable IC consequences.

  2. 11

    Capture & Imprisonment

    Characters may be arrested, captured, questioned, or imprisoned as part of RP. Captivity should remain interactive and reasonable and should not be used solely to prevent another player from participating in the server for excessive periods.

  3. 12

    Crime Is Roleplay

    Theft, murder, banditry, assassination, corruption, kidnapping, and other criminal activities are permitted when supported by reasonable IC motivation and RP.

    Characters who commit crimes should also expect appropriate IC consequences from guards, authorities, factions, bounty hunters, or other characters.

  4. 13

    No Combat Logging

    Do not intentionally disconnect or leave the game to avoid an active RP situation, combat, capture, arrest, or other immediate IC consequence.

    If you crash or disconnect unexpectedly, return to the scene when reasonably possible.

Rules 14–15

Authority and scale

  1. 14

    Faction Authority Must Be Established IC

    Military ranks, political authority, noble titles, religious positions, faction leadership, and similar positions of influence must be established through character creation or earned through RP.

    Players may not simply declare themselves to hold significant authority within the world.

  2. 15

    Large-Scale Conflict Requires Staff Coordination

    Wars, sieges, settlement attacks, faction raids, coups, and other actions capable of significantly altering the server world require staff coordination and approval.

    Normal fights, arrests, political disputes, and smaller conflicts do not require staff approval simply because characters belong to opposing factions.

Rules 16–17

The spirit of it

  1. 16

    Skyrim Mechanics Do Not Override RP

    Something being mechanically possible in Skyrim does not automatically make it acceptable within RP. Players are expected to consider what reasonably makes sense for their character and the situation.

  2. 17

    Prioritize Roleplay Over Winning

    DragonBreak is a collaborative roleplaying server. Do not approach RP solely with the goal of winning every fight, argument, political conflict, or encounter.

    Allow your character to face consequences, setbacks, defeats, and changes. The actions of other characters should matter.

Rules 18–19 · No IC exception

Hard limits

  1. 18

    No Sexual Roleplay / ERP

    Sexual roleplay and Erotic Roleplay (ERP) are prohibited on DragonBreak. This includes sexually explicit interactions, descriptions, emotes, voice roleplay, or using in-game actions or animations to depict sexual activity.

    Romance between characters is permitted, but sexual activity must remain off-screen and may not be actively roleplayed. If a scene would become sexual, fade to black and move on.

  2. 19

    All Characters Must Be Adults

    All player characters on DragonBreak must be 18 years of age or older.

    Players may not create, portray, or roleplay as a minor, regardless of whether the character is involved in romantic or sexual content.

About these rules

These nineteen are the agreed rules of play. Where a rule and a staff ruling disagree, the ruling stands for that scene and the page gets corrected afterwards.

DragonBreak Online is a fan-made roleplay server for The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition.
