Starting Out in Alpha

What the alpha is

The DragonBreak Online alpha opens on Saturday 3 October 2026 at 05:00 UTC (midnight Central US time).

  • Free. The game costs nothing to play.
  • Open to everyone in the Discord. Membership of the DragonBreak Discord is all you need to log in. You must be 18 or older.
  • Long term, with no wipe. After the one-time reset at launch, your characters, skills and belongings carry on.
  • Bruma only, for now. The alpha takes place in and around Bruma. The border doors and roads out of the region are closed, and anyone who crosses the border is brought back. More of the world opens as the alpha grows.

Installing

The DragonBreak launcher does the work: it keeps its own copy of Skyrim, installs the mods and the multiplayer client into it, keeps them up to date, and starts the game.

  1. Install Skyrim Special Edition from Steam, start it once, reach the main menu and quit. You do not need the Anniversary Edition upgrade.
  2. Join the Discord from the home page.
  3. Sign in with Discord on the home page. Your profile page has the launcher download.
  4. Run the launcher, tell it where Skyrim is, and let it install everything. The full modlist is also a Nexus collection, if you would rather fetch the mods yourself.

What your PC needs

SystemWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
GameOnly the Steam version of Skyrim Special Edition is supported (the launcher checks for version 1.6.1170).
Memory16 GB is the least that works, with the Windows page file left on System managed. Close browsers and other big programs while you play. 32 GB is better for big dungeons with a full party: games on 16 GB machines have closed in the busiest dungeons with nearly all the memory in use.
DownloadThe DragonBreak client and files come to about 1.1 GB, on top of the mod collection.

Logging in

In the launcher, click Discord Login at the top. Your browser opens; approve the sign-in there and go back to the launcher, which then shows your Discord name. Press Play. There is no separate account or password: your Discord account is your DragonBreak account.

The reset at launch

Once, at launch, every character starts fresh. The server closes at 04:30 UTC on 3 October for the reset and opens again at 05:00.

ResetSkills, levels, gold, bank accounts and items go back to the starting kit, and every container is emptied. Learned spells, schools of magic and recipes are reset, and so are faith, house claims, business ownership, guild membership and any rite in progress. Vampirism, lycanthropy and the vampire's disease are lifted: everyone starts mortal, with their mortal look.
KeptNames, appearance, race and character slots.

After this there is no wipe.

For our playtesters

  • A second character slot, on top of any others you have.
  • Double skill progress for 24 hours, starting from your first login after the alpha opens. Log in by 05:00 UTC on 10 October to claim it. Type /boost to see how long it has left.

Supporters get a few extra conveniences, such as extra character slots; ask in our Discord.

When something goes wrong

/bug <what happened>Report a bug in game, for example /bug the inn door won't open. It saves where you are and what is around you, so send it while the problem is in front of you.
/ticket <pk|mod|report> <what happened>A private ticket with staff on Discord: pk to ask for a player kill, mod for moderation help, report to report a player. Bugs go to /bug.
Report a ProblemWhen the game closes or won't start: in the launcher, click the gear, then Troubleshooting and Report a Problem. It sends your logs, including the newest crash log, to staff. Send it straight away.
/unstuckStuck in the scenery? This takes you to the respawn point of your area, once every 25 minutes.

Known issues at launch

  • Beast forms look human to others. For now, other players see a Vampire Lord or a werewolf in their human shape. Your own form works as usual.
  • Enemies can float, sink or slide in big fights, and a hit can miss one. Type /bug while it happens.
  • Dual casting does not give a dual cast's extra strength yet.
  • Summon Will-o-the-Wisp is switched off until a crash is fixed.
  • Dragon bone and scales come only from a slain dragon, and there are no dragons in the alpha yet.
  • Weapon racks, plaques and display holders drop what you put on them. Pick it back up.
