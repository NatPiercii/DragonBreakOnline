Holds & Factions

The holds and their offices, the houses you can be granted or rent, the treasuries that pay for it all, and every clan, guild, secret society, pack and Daedric cult you can swear to, with the ranks you climb inside each one.

The land at the alpha opening

It is 4E 211. The Legion has withdrawn from Skyrim, and its nine Holds rule themselves, each under its Jarl. Bruma, on the Cyrodiil side of the Jerall Mountains, is a county of the Empire under its Count. The Orc strongholds answer only to their Chieftains.

Held to Bruma

For the alpha, everyone plays in the County of Bruma. Every hold, stronghold and faction on this page exists, but only Bruma's halls, houses and offices can be reached until the rest of the land opens. A guildhall outside Bruma is listed as not reachable yet.

A fresh start

The alpha opens on Thursday 1 October at 05:00 UTC with a one-time reset. Every house and property claim, every rented house, every business and every faction membership is cleared, so all houses can be granted again and every faction starts from an empty roster. Each hold's treasury opens with 10,000 gold, and no property tax or wage is paid until a hold's officials set one.

The Realm map

The Realm tab of the faction panel (F3) maps the County of Bruma: the city of Bruma, which is its capital, the towns of Applewatch and Aleswell, the village of Greenwood, and the forts of Pale Pass and Fort Caractacus. The Imperial Legion holds the two forts; the County holds the rest.

Strongholds stand apart

Inside an Orc stronghold only its own officials manage property, locks and notices. A hold's officials have no say there, and a stronghold's have none outside it.

How factions work

A faction is a group of players with its own ranks. You can belong to several at once, and each of your characters joins separately.

Open the faction menu

Press F3 to see your factions, their members, who is online right now, and any invites waiting for you. The panel also holds the Realm map and, for rulers and leaders, the treasury.

Joining

Factions grow by invitation. Look at a player and press X: the menu lists Invite to … for every faction where your rank lets you invite. An invite expires after 2 minutes, and you join at the lowest rank. Server staff name a faction's first leader; after that the leader runs it.

Chat commands

  • /faction menu open the menu
  • /faction list list the factions
  • /faction accept accept an invite
  • /faction invite invite a player
  • /faction hall where a faction has its seat
  • /faction storage leaders: record where the faction keeps its things

Seats in Bruma

Castle Bruma is the seat of the County, and the Imperial Legion and the Thalmor keep their offices there too. The Synod meets in the Bruma Synod Conclave, the College of Whispers in Frostcrag Spire above the city, and the Fighters Guild in its Bruma hall.

Faction storage

A leader can record a house or container they own as the faction's storage, so members can find it with /faction hall. It grants no access by itself: lock it and cut keys for whoever should reach it.

Leaders are not Jarls

A faction leader runs a player faction. Jarls, Counts and Chieftains hold hold offices, a separate system described below. A faction rank called Jarl or Guard carries none of an office's powers. One link runs the other way: a Jarl or Count also speaks for their hold's faction in matters of the realm, such as its capital, peace treaties, its tax and its wages.

The seven roles

Every rank title, from Harbinger to Footpad, maps to one of seven roles. The role decides what the rank may do; the title is the lore.

Leader

1 per faction
InviteKickSet ranks

Full control. Naming a new leader steps the old one down to the rank below, so a faction never has two.

Officer

InviteRemove lower ranks

Recruits and removes members who rank beneath them. Guard Captains and Thanes both hold this role.

Sergeant

Invite

Brings new blood in; cannot remove anyone.

Mage

The faction's spellcasters. A standing role with no management powers.

Blacksmith

Max 3

Forges the faction's own armour and weapons.

Tailor

Max 3

Makes the faction's own robes, cloaks and other clothing at the loom or tanning rack.

Member

Sworn and counted. The bottom rungs of every ladder.

Many factions have gear of their own, such as the Legion's armour or the Thalmor's robes. Forged pieces can be crafted only by that faction's Blacksmiths and clothing only by its Tailors, or by its leader; anyone else is refused and keeps their materials.

Numbers are the most seats a hold or stronghold's own officials may fill between them, however many of them are naming. Seats are filled with /appoint and emptied with /dismiss: whoever may name a seat may also dismiss it, and both work whether the player is online or not. An office belongs to the player, not to one character, and a player holds one seat per hold or stronghold. Server staff name the rulers and the seats marked –. By Skyrim's law a Jarl must be a Nord or an Imperial; only the server owners can make an exception. /officials shows who rules where.

Guard powers

Every official except a Court Mage, Shaman or Wise-Woman keeps the peace. From the X menu they can restrain a player without asking and search one who allows it, and at a jail's cell door they can lock a player in for 5 minutes to 2 hours. Time is served only while the prisoner is online and inside.

Property

The ruler and the Stewards (in a stronghold, the Chieftain and the Banes) grant houses, shops and containers, take them back, rename them and change their locks. They also let empty houses out for rent.

Notice boards

Officials post Hold Notices for free and may take any notice down from their hold's boards. They can post hunting contracts, paid from the hold's treasury, and a disputed commission goes before them to settle.

The hold's tax

The Jarl or Count and the Stewards set the hold's share of every inn bed rented in it, from 0 to 30% with /tax <percent>. Until they set one, the hold takes 10%.

The treasury

A hold's treasury is kept in the bank, not in a chest anyone could break into. A Jarl or Count, and every faction leader, sees the treasuries they answer for at any bank counter and may pay their own gold in. No one takes gold out: the realm spends it.

Gold comes in

  • Half of every paid post on the hold's notice boards
  • Rent on houses the hold lets out, and the deposit of a tenant who is evicted
  • The hold's share of inn bed rent, and all of it at an inn nobody owns
  • The weekly property tax on its land
  • The fee and duty on commissions posted at its boards
  • Postage on pigeons sent from the hold

Gold goes out

Hunting contracts: the reward is set aside from the treasury when the notice goes up and returns to it if nobody finishes the work. Wages: paid every week to the ranks the leader chooses.

Faction treasuries

A hold's faction shares its hold's treasury. Every other faction has one of its own, which its leader can pay into at a bank.

The weekly reckoning

Every Sunday at 00:00 UTC the realm settles its accounts. Each claimed property pays tax to the faction that owns the land it stands on, and wages are paid into members' bank accounts, so nobody needs to be online. Each faction's last reckoning is shown in the faction panel.

Property tax

A faction's leader, or a hold's Jarl or Count, sets the weekly tax on homes on its land, from 0 to 30% of a property's value. A property is valued at 2,000 gold until the officials who manage it assess it; they may reassess it once a week, to no more than double or half. The tax comes out of the owner's bank account. Unpaid tax falls overdue and the owner is told.

Wages

The leader sets a weekly wage for each rank. A hold pays its officials by office; any other faction pays its members by rank. No one is paid from a treasury their own account controls, and no one sets the wage of their own rank. Each week's wages are capped at three quarters of the week's income and a quarter of the treasury; what cannot be paid is owed and paid first the next week.

Houses, inns and business

Property is granted, not taken. Walking up to an empty house does not make it yours.

Granted homes

A hold's ruler or Steward (a stronghold's Chieftain or Bane) claims a house, shop or container and grants it to a player from the property menu (X on its door). A player may hold up to 8 properties across their characters.

Owning property

From the same menu the owner locks it, names it, cuts keys, hands it to someone else or gives it up. A locked property stays shut to everyone, the owner included, until the owner, a key holder or an official who manages it unlocks it from the menu. Keys are named after the property.

Renting a house

Officials let an empty house out at its door for a deposit (up to 20,000 gold) and a weekly rent (up to 5,000). At the door, /property shows the terms and /property interest puts your name down. If they offer it to you, you have 48 hours to come back and say /property accept: you pay the deposit and the first week, and the house and its keys are yours to live in.

Paying rent

Pay at the door with /property pay, up to 8 weeks at once; the rent goes to the hold's treasury. When rent falls overdue the officials may remind you, give you a week's grace, or evict you, and the hold keeps the deposit. Leave in good standing with /property leave and the deposit comes back. A rented house cannot be handed on to someone else.

Inn beds

Use a free bed at an inn to rent it: 10 gold for 24 hours, unless the inn's owner has set another price. The rent goes to the inn's owner less the hold's share; an inn nobody owns pays it all to the hold. You rent one bed at a time across all your characters. An inn's owner can keep one of its beds as their own, to sleep in for free. In Bruma, try the Jerall View Inn or the Restful Watchman.

Running a business

The owner of an inn or shop can stand inside and open it as a business with /business open <name>. Its ledger book sits on the counter: the week's and the month's takings, the price of a bed (1 to 200 gold), a logbook, and up to 12 staff. /business collect pays out the takings.

Capitals, treaties and war

A faction's leader, or a hold's Jarl or Count, speaks for it in the realm from the faction panel.

Capital

A hold chooses one of its own territories as its capital. Any other faction chooses a territory it holds, or makes the spot where its leader stands its seat, such as a guildhall. A capital can move once a week, and not during a war.

Peace treaties

Two leaders who are not at war can swear peace for 1 to 8 weeks: one offers from the War tab, the other accepts. While the treaty holds, neither side can declare war on the other.

War Not open yet

The rules of war and of raids on another faction's land are built, but both stay closed during the alpha while the holds and factions are set up. The War tab says so.

Parties and raids

A party is for the moment, a faction is for good. A party sets out on expeditions together and shares the gold found inside. Partymates deal only a fifth of their damage to each other and cannot finish each other off while down.

Party

Up to 6 players. /party invite, /party accept, /party leave.

Raid

7 to 12 players. Skill gain is halved for everyone while the raid lasts, except inside a raid ruin.

Leader

The leader removes members from the X menu on a player, or with /party kick.

Every faction

Ranks read top to bottom, highest first. Secret factions show their roster only to their own members.
