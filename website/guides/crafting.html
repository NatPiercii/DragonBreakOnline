Crafting and Trades

Every bench in Tamriel belongs to a trade. Take the trade up, and your rank decides how fine your work can be; where you stand and who you are decide what you may make.

Trades and their stations

Eight trades on the Wheel of Skills each own their stations, and only someone who holds the trade may use them. The first time you set your hand to one, you take the trade up for one point and start at Novice. If every one of your 300 points is in use, mark a skill to fall in your skills menu (K) first.

A craft you finish at a real bench is work for its trade, and a costlier piece counts for more. The Leveling Guide has the numbers.

Your rank sets the ceiling

Tempering

Only a Blacksmith can improve gear at a grindstone or workbench, and the Blacksmith’s tier caps how far:

Blacksmith tierImprove gear up to
NoviceFine
ApprenticeSuperior
JourneymanExquisite
ExpertEpic
MasterLegendary

Enchanting

Only an Enchanter can lay an enchantment, and its strength is measured against the strongest enchantment of the same effect found anywhere in the world: half of it at Novice, three quarters at Apprentice, the full strength at Journeyman, one and a half times at Expert and twice at Master.

Alchemy

Only an Alchemist can brew. Your tier decides how strong a potion or poison comes out, from weak at Novice to the strongest brews at Master, and each brew is work for your Alchemist. Using an alchemy lab also shows you which potions the ingredients you carry can make, and which two to combine.

Crafting follows the land

Gear is made where it belongs. What you may make depends on the province you stand in, and during the alpha that is Cyrodiil. In Cyrodiil the land allows the common work found everywhere and the styles of Cyrodiil and its neighbours, Imperial, Cyrodilic and Elven work among them. The arms of Skyrim, Morrowind and Solstheim are not made here.

A people can always make their own, wherever they stand:

RaceMakes its own anywhere
OrcOrcish
NordDraugr and Nordic work, stalhrim and Skaal

Bretons and Argonians wait for High Rock and Black Marsh. A craft the land refuses costs you nothing: your materials come back when you step away from the bench.

Most forge recipes above iron are not open yet in the alpha, so for now several of these styles cannot be forged anywhere.

Cooking is free everywhere. Every dish can be cooked in every land.

Mining

Strike an ore vein and a round begins: 30 seconds to land six clean strikes while the marker crosses the mark. The higher your Miner tier, the wider the mark. The server judges every strike. The table lists what the Bruma region holds.

Miner tierWhat you can mineYield
NoviceCopper, iron, corundum; sea saltx1
ApprenticeSilverx1.2
JourneymanMoonstone, gold; ruby, sapphirex1.4
ExpertMalachitex1.7
MasterEbony; diamondx2
  • Ore: a copper or iron vein gives 3 ore at Novice and 6 at Master; corundum, silver and moonstone 2 to 4; gold, malachite and ebony 1 to 2.
  • Sea salt deposits give 4 salt piles at Novice and 8 at Master, and one round in ten turns up frost, fire or void salts as well. They are small and easy to miss, so they glow for Miners while they have salt to give.
  • Gem geodes give the gem they are named for, one or two of them.

A vein somebody has worked out rests for 45 minutes, for everyone, and only one person can work a vein at a time. A failed or abandoned round rests the vein for you alone, for two minutes.

Chopping wood

A chopping block works the same way: a 30-second round of clean strikes, 4 of them at Novice and 20 at Master, for 3 to 8 firewood. A split block rests for 10 minutes, for everyone.

Cooking

Only a Cook may use cooking pots, spits and ovens. A cooked meal fills you far better than raw food: a meal eases your hunger by 35, a snack by 15, a drink by 8 and a raw ingredient by only 4. A dish with more ingredients counts for more Cook work, so the stew teaches more than the grilled leeks. Your Cook tier does not change what you can cook.

The Draught of Revival

An Alchemist of Expert tier who has learned its recipe can brew the Draught of Revival at any alchemy lab from troll fat, fly amanita and yellow cinnabar polypore. The recipe turns up now and then in a boss’s chest. Pour the draught on a fallen player with E to bring them back.

Breaking things down

What you no longer need can be broken back into materials at the station of its trade: metal gear at a smelter (Blacksmith), hides and leather at a tanning rack (Skinner), clothing at a loom (Tailor), and books at a writing desk or the Scholars’ Ledger in the Synod Conclave (Scholar).

Your tier in that tradeOf the item’s materials, you get back
NoviceA quarter
Apprentice35%
JourneymanHalf
Expert60%
MasterThree quarters
  • You always get at least one of the main material. Breaking down metal gear needs the Blacksmith tier that works its metal.
  • A bound book gives two sheets of paper and a leather strip; a note, one sheet.
  • Worn gear is never offered, and neither is anything enchanted, named, tempered, poisoned or charged.
  • Dragon gear gives back its other materials, never its dragon bone or scales.

Books on smithing

The skill books that teach smithing teach a Blacksmith here too: reading one adds to your Blacksmith progress, once for each book, within the usual hourly and daily limits. Someone who is not a smith learns nothing from them.

What no forge makes

  • Dragon bone and dragon scales come only from a slain dragon. No loot, shop, reward or breakdown gives them.
  • Artifacts are never crafted and never found as loot; they come to the champions of the realm through the story, from the staff. You can still temper one you hold.
  • A faction’s own gear is made only by that faction’s smiths and tailors, or by its leader.

Rope

Anyone carrying a Rope can tie up another player: press X on them and choose Tie Up. Someone who is down is tied at once; anyone standing is asked first, and nobody who says no is tied. Each knot uses one rope. Guards and officials keep their shackles.

  • The captor can Untie, Leave Tied Here, and Lead the captive along again.
  • Rope is weaker than shackles. Once your captor has been more than 10 metres away for 30 seconds, the knots loosen and /struggle gets much easier. After 5 minutes alone in all, the rope slips off by itself.
  • A friend standing right beside you can press X and choose Cut Free: five seconds, and neither of you may move.

No shop or recipe offers Rope yet.
