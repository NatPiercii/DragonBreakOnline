Vampires & Werewolves

Two ancient curses, both drawn from the lore of Tamriel. How you catch them, how you survive the turning, what they give, and what they cost.

VampirismLycanthropy
Alpha launch

Everyone starts mortal

When the alpha opens on Thursday 1 October at 05:00 UTC, every character begins mortal. Vampirism, lycanthropy and the diseases that carry them are lifted, every mortal face comes back, blood ranks and Great Hunt renown start from nothing, and the Blood Crown lies unclaimed. Whoever takes up a curse after that does it the way this guide describes.

Shared world

One sky for everyone

The server keeps the clock, the calendar, the moons and the weather. When it is night for you, it is night for every player in Tamriel.

Day and night

A day in Tamriel passes in four real hours. Night runs from 8 in the evening to 6 in the morning, and sunrise burns the same vampire on every screen at once.

Moon phases

The moons wax and wane together, three days to a phase. They are full for three days in every twenty-four, about twelve real hours in every four real days, and for the whole server at once. Type /time to see the phase.

Weather

Weather is shared by everyone in the same region. Cloud halves the sun's burn on a vampire, and rain or snow cuts it to a quarter.

Molag Bal's curse

Vampirism

Vampirism begins as a disease, Sanguinare Vampiris. Left alone, it ends in undeath.

  1. Infection

    Bitten

    Every hit from a vampire carries a 10% chance of Sanguinare Vampiris, and so does being fed on by one while you are awake.

  2. 3 in-game days

    Incubation

    Three in-game days of your own play, about twelve real hours online; time logged out does not count. Still curable: drink a Cure Disease potion, or complete a prayer to your own god, if your god is not a Daedric Prince.

  3. The fever peaks

    Blood Fever

    A trial of five strikes: strike as the pulse crosses the mark. Three true strikes and you rise. Fail and you die, and the disease burns out with you.

  4. Turned

    Stage 1 vampire

    You wake undead, but your gifts sleep until your first meal. Go six in-game hours of play without blood and your body begins to wither.

The other road

Molag Bal's Embrace

A voluntary rite at a shrine of Molag Bal. Touch the shrine and choose Perform the Rite: the panel shows you what it can cost, and only Kneel begins it. It asks four true strikes of five. Survive it and you rise a pure-blood, gifts awake at once, with half the sun's burn and half the weakness to fire. A turned vampire may kneel too. Not all who kneel rise again, as with the Daughters of Coldharbour: failing kills you, about one time in three it is permanent death for your character, and the shrine will not hear you again for 24 hours.

If your game crashes or your connection drops during a rite, the rounds you already played decide it. Behind, the rite is lost, but a disconnect never ends a character for good. Even or ahead, the rite is called off and nobody dies, though after Molag Bal's Embrace or the Great Hunt that shrine still waits 24 hours.

One stage per in-game day without feeding; a higher blood rank slows the climb. A pure-blood takes half the sun and half the fire. The sun wears you down to a sliver of health but never past it, and leaves you easy prey. Cover up: a hood or helmet, body clothing, gloves and boots together keep up to 80% of the sun off you (a circlet covers nothing). Silver you can neither wear nor strike with.

Your gifts grow with your thirst: Vampiric Drain, Vampire's Servant and Vampire's Sight from stage 1, Vampire's Seduction from stage 2, Embrace of Shadows at stage 4. The thirstier you are, the stronger your gifts and the worse you burn. Ordinary food barely eases a vampire's hunger. Your thirst, rank and powers are on the Vampire tab of the Skills menu (K).

Blood, crown and cure

Feeding

Activate a fresh humanoid body, slain within the last ten minutes, or a player lying downed, to drink. A captive with bound hands you feed on from the X menu. Feeding takes a few seconds and breaks if you move away or are struck. It returns you to Stage 1.

A living person gives blood once per in-game day, loses health to you, and may catch the disease. Feeding can leave blood on your mouth and chin, most often on a young or hungry vampire; it stays until you wash it off in water.

Asking for blood

Anyone awake who is not a vampire can be asked: choose Feed in the X menu. They have 20 seconds to Offer Your Neck or Resist / Flee, and no answer is a refusal. You can ask once a minute, and someone who refused you cannot be asked again for five minutes.

Blood and rank

Blood earns rank (type /blood): 5 for a fresh body, 20 for a living person, bound or willing, and 30 for a player's body if you brought them down yourself (nothing if someone else did). The same person counts once a real day, never a partymate or your own characters, and companions and summons give nothing.

  • Fledgling0
  • Vampire100
  • Nightstalker300
  • Mistwalker700
  • Master Vampire1500

Each rank feeds faster, from 12 seconds down to 6, and hardens you: at Master Vampire the sun burns 40% less, thirst climbs 40% slower and your hits land 10% heavier at night. From Nightstalker you can Feed Deeply on a living person: twice as long, the thirst held off longer, faster recovery for six in-game hours and half again the blood, and your victim blacks out. A cure starts the next vampire at Fledgling again; becoming a pure-blood keeps what you earned.

The Blood Crown

Molag Bal grants the Vampire Lord gift to one champion at a time. When it lies unclaimed, the next vampire to rise pure-blooded from his Embrace takes it. Any vampire who slays the holder takes it and becomes pure-blooded, so the clans are always at war over it.

As a Vampire Lord you fly and drain, raise the dead, summon a gargoyle, and scatter into bats or mist. For now, other players see you in your mortal form, without your gear, while you are transformed, and they do not see your spells' effects, though the spells still strike them.

The cure

  • The rite of Arkay or Stendarr: carry a filled black soul gem to a shrine of either god, touch it, choose Perform the Rite, then Offer the soul gem. The gem drinks the curse and is spent, you are mortal again, and any rank you earned goes with it. A Blood Crown passes from you.
  • The same rite lifts lycanthropy.
  • Still in the fever? Pray instead: the rite is for those already turned.

Hircine's gift

Lycanthropy

Lycanthropy passes by bite as Sanies Lupinus. Those who survive it belong to the Huntsman.

  1. Infection

    Bitten

    Every hit from a werewolf in beast form carries a 2% chance of Sanies Lupinus, far rarer than a vampire's bite.

  2. 3 in-game days

    Incubation

    The same window of your own play, and the same cures: a Cure Disease potion, or a completed prayer to your own god, if your god is not a Daedric Prince.

  3. The fever peaks

    Hircine's Hunt

    The trial of the beast: five strikes, three true to survive. Fail and you die.

  4. Turned

    Werewolf

    The beast lives under your skin from now on.

The other road

The rite of Hircine

Seek the beast willingly at a shrine of Hircine: touch it, choose Perform the Rite, then Run the Hunt. It asks four true strikes of five. Outrun him and he may mark you with Sanies Lupinus, whose fever then runs its course as after a bite, but he marks only about one in four who outrun him. The Huntsman does not forgive weakness: failing kills you, and about one time in three it is permanent death. Fail or go unmarked, and his shrine will not hear you again for 24 hours.

The beast

Beast form

Change at will once per in-game day for 150 seconds. Activate a fresh body, beast or person, to feed: it takes a few seconds, adds 30 seconds, heals you and eases your hunger. Your claws are your weapons, with the Howl of Terror and the Totem of the Hunt on your Shout key.

0 s150 s+30 s

For now, other players see you in your human shape while you are in beast form. On your own screen you are the beast.

Drawbacks

  • Weakness to silver: it strikes you 50% harder, and you cannot wear it or strike with it.
  • No gear in beast form.
  • Anyone close by sees you change.
  • Your Howl of Terror is heard by everyone in the land, echoing through the region you are in.
  • The beast can take you when you least want it (see the full moon, below).

Renown · the Great Hunt

A werewolf climbs by living as one (type /hunt). Feeding in beast form earns 5 for an animal, 10 for a person, 60 for a player's body, but only if you brought them down yourself, outdoors and away from the city (otherwise it earns nothing), once a real day per person, never a partymate or your own characters. A kill in beast form earns 2, though never a player, and so does each change, up to the day's allowance.

  • Fledgling0
  • Prowler100
  • Hunter300
  • Blood-Howler700
  • Elder1500

Rank lengthens the change and every feed, lets it come more often, and hardens the beast. An Elder holds the beast for 300 seconds, gains 60 seconds from each feed, changes up to three times a day, takes 20% less and deals 20% more in beast form, and is forced into the change far less often. Companions and summons earn nothing, fed on or slain. A cure starts the next werewolf at Fledgling again.

Pack leader

Each pack has one leader. A packmate who brings the leader down, both in beast form, takes the place. The leader is also free of the daily limit and is never forced to change. A werewolf in no pack is a Lone Wolf, and a pack takes you in only by invitation.

Your rank, pack, changes left today and how restless the beast is are on the Werewolf tab of the Skills menu (K).

The full moon
Night+Full moon+Outdoors→ each in-game hour,1 in 10chance you change whether you want to or not.

This follows the old full-moon curse of Daggerfall and the rule of the Ring of Hircine. But the moon is not the only danger. The beast can tear free at any time, indoors or out, and the hungrier you are, the likelier it is, more so at night and most of all on a full-moon night. A pack's leader is spared both, and a higher rank in the Great Hunt holds the beast back better. Stay fed if you have secrets to keep.

One curse only

No one is both vampire and werewolf. A bite from the other kind does not take, Molag Bal will not embrace a werewolf, and Hircine will not hunt the dead. To change sides, be cured first.
