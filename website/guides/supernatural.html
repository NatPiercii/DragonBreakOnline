Vampires & Werewolves

Two ancient curses, both drawn from the lore of Tamriel. How you catch them, how you survive the turning, what they give, and what they cost.

VampirismLycanthropy
Shared world

One sky for everyone

The server keeps the clock, the calendar, the moons and the weather. When it is night for you, it is night for every player in Tamriel.

Day and night

Sunrise burns the same vampire on every screen at once.

Moon phases

A full moon rises for the whole server, not just for you.

Weather

Storms and overcast skies are shared, and they matter to anyone who fears the sun.

Molag Bal's curse

Vampirism

Vampirism begins as a disease, Sanguinare Vampiris. Left alone, it ends in undeath.

  1. Infection

    Bitten

    Every hit from a vampire carries a 10% chance of Sanguinare Vampiris.

  2. 3 in-game days

    Incubation

    Still curable: a Cure Disease potion or a shrine blessing ends it.

  3. The fever peaks

    Blood Fever

    A timed ritual trial. Fail and you die, and the disease burns out with you.

  4. Turned

    Stage 1 vampire

    You wake undead. From here, hunger sets the pace.

The other road

Molag Bal's Embrace

A voluntary rite at a shrine of Molag Bal, and the only way to pure blood. Few who take it survive, as with the Daughters of Coldharbour: failing the rite can mean permanent death for your character.

Blood, crown and cure

Feeding

Feed on a humanoid who is restrained, downed, or freshly slain: use the X menu on them, or activate the corpse. Feeding returns you to Stage 1.

The Blood Crown

Molag Bal grants the Vampire Lord gift to one champion in Cyrodiil at a time. Any vampire who slays the holder takes it, so the clans are always at war over it.

The cure

  • Falion's rite: bring a filled black soul gem to a shrine of Arkay or Stendarr.
  • Lycanthropy also cures vampirism, at its own price.

Hircine's gift

Lycanthropy

Lycanthropy passes by bite as Sanies Lupinus. Those who survive it belong to the Huntsman.

  1. Infection

    Bitten

    A werewolf's bite can pass on Sanies Lupinus.

  2. 3 in-game days

    Incubation

    The same curable window: a Cure Disease potion or a shrine blessing.

  3. The fever peaks

    Hircine's Hunt

    The trial of the beast. Fail and you die.

  4. Turned

    Werewolf

    The beast lives under your skin from now on.

The other road

The rite of Hircine

Seek the change willingly at a shrine of Hircine. The Huntsman does not forgive weakness: failing the rite risks permanent death.

The beast

Beast form

Change at will once per in-game day for 150 seconds. Each corpse you feed on adds 30 seconds and heals you.

0 s150 s+30 s

Drawbacks

  • Weakness to silver.
  • No gear in beast form.
  • A change someone witnesses marks you.
  • Hircine claims your soul when you die.

Pack leader

Each pack has one leader. Defeat the current leader in beast form to take the place. By old tradition, only the pack leader wears the white coat.

The full moon
Night+Full moon+Outdoors→ each in-game hour,1 in 10chance you change whether you want to or not.

This follows the old full-moon curse of Daggerfall and the rule of the Ring of Hircine. Stay indoors on a full-moon night if you have secrets to keep.

One curse only

No one is both vampire and werewolf. Taking one cures the other: Hircine's blood drives out Molag Bal's, and Molag Bal's drives out Hircine's.
