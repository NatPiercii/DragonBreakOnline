Commands and Systems

How to talk

Anything you type that is not a command is spoken aloud, and only people close enough hear it. These change how far your voice carries or mark it as something other than speech.

(just type)Normal speech, heard by anyone nearby.
/lowQuietly. A shorter reach than normal speech.
/whisperBarely audible, for the person beside you.
/wideLoudly, to a room rather than a conversation.
/shoutAs far as your voice will carry.
/me <action>An action, written as "Argosh draws his axe". /melow and /melong change the range.
/my <thing>The same, but about something of yours: "Argosh's axe is notched".
/do <detail>A detail of the scene with no name attached.
/looc <text>Out of character, to people nearby. /ooc is the same.
/pm <player> <text>A private message, wherever they are.

Voice chat is separate: hold V to speak and tap Left Alt to switch between Whisper, Talk and Shout. A whisper only carries to whoever you are facing; a shout reaches sixty metres.

The keys that matter

Much of the game is reached with a key or by walking up to something, not by typing.

UThe player menu: every command below, grouped by topic, with a line on what each does. The same topics as /help.
KYour skills: the Wheel of Skills, your ranks, and the magic schools on the Arcane Arts page.
XOn another player: introduce yourself, trade, inspect them or invite them to your party.
HHide your face or show it again.
F3Your factions.
BThe emote wheel.

Letters by pigeon

Use a notice board to write a letter and send it by pigeon to anyone you have met, even if they are offline. It waits for them at the notice boards. A letter holds up to 240 characters and costs 5 to 50 gold depending on how far it flies, and you can send one every 35 minutes. You can also send one with /ledger letter wherever a ledger lies: at a notice board, in your own home, or where a court or guild keeps one.

/pigeonblock <name|#TAG>Stop letters from someone reaching you, or let them through again.
/sign <unsigned|a name|clear>Choose how your next letter is signed: unsigned, or clear to sign it with your own name again. Signing another's name takes a Scholar of Journeyman or above, and below Master the reader may notice the hand does not match.

Your character

What you are, what you know, and how you are doing.

/statusYour hunger, rest, and anything else weighing on your character, including when you have a level point to spend.
/level [health|magicka|stamina] [n]Your character level and progress. With a stat, it spends your points on it.
/boostFor playtesters: your double skill progress and the time it has left.
/spellsYour spellbook: every spell you have studied, and the ones you have prepared. You change your prepared spells in the Synod Conclave in Bruma or beside a Scholars' Ledger.
/teachTeach a spell to a player beside you (Arcane Arts or Priest at Expert).
/hungerHow hungry you are.
/restHow long Well Rested and Well Fed have left.
/deity [name]Open the deity menu. /deity <name> at that god's shrine turns to them by hand.
/prayPray anywhere to a faith that needs no shrine (the Hist, the Ancestors and the others).
/offer <gold>An offering of 5 to 1,000 gold to your god, made at their shrine (anywhere, for a faith that needs none). Half keeps the town's temple, and for the next hour your god listens more closely.
/riteAt the shrine of Molag Bal (the Embrace), Hircine (the Great Hunt), or Arkay or Stendarr (the cure, with a filled black soul gem). Touching the shrine opens the same choice as a panel.
/name <name>Name your character, if it came out of creation without one.
/whoamiYour name, tag and ids.

Groups and getting about

Dungeons are claimed by a party, not by one person.

/party invite|accept|decline|leave|kickGroup up for dungeons: six to a party, and up to twelve in a raid at half skill gain. If the leader leaves, whoever has been in it longest takes over; a party down to one person breaks up.
/expedition leaveIn an Ayleid ruin, go home to Bruma. Expeditions set out from the board in the Synod Conclave or the Fighters Guild.
/dungeonDungeon claims: what is taken, and what rests for you.
/playersWho is online.
/playtestWhat part of the world is open.
/unstuckTakes you to the respawn point of the area you are in. You can use it once every 25 minutes, and not while you are down, bound or carried, in a jail or serving a sentence, or within a minute of fighting another player.

The world

Work, money and who rules where.

/boardOpen the notice board you are standing at.
/contractTake or post hunting work. The Contracts tab of the expedition board shows the same work.
/commission post|take|done|refuse|cancel|abandon|ruleWork posted at a notice board, with the reward held by the board until it is done; the town's officials settle disputes. On its own it lists the board's commissions.
/bankAt a bank counter: your account, the same in every town. Deposit, withdraw, or pay into a treasury you answer for.
/businessWhere you stand in a business: its ledger (the ledger book opens the same panel).
/property [mine|interest|accept|pay|leave]At a house's door: its rent terms, asking to rent it, moving in, paying rent and leaving. /property mine lists the houses you manage as an official. Officials also list, offer, remind, give grace and evict.
/taxThe hold's tax on rents where you stand. Officials set it with /tax <percent>.
/ledger [page|<name>|note|group|letter|forget]Your ledger of everyone you have met, read at a notice board, in your home, or where a court or guild keeps one: notes, groups, letters by pigeon, and forgetting someone.
/timeThe time, the date and the moons.
/wildlifeWhat roams the wilds.
/championsNamed beasts currently abroad.
/officials [zone]Who rules where.
/faction [menu|list|accept|invite]Your factions (F3 opens the menu).
/appoint <player|#TAG> <zone> <rank>Name someone to an office, online or offline, if your own office allows it. A Jarl or the Count of Bruma names up to 5 Stewards, 2 Court Mages, a Guard Captain and 20 Guards. A Chieftain names up to 5 Banes, a Shaman, a Wise-Woman, a Stronghold Guard Commander and 20 Stronghold Guards. A Guard Captain or Hold Commander names Guards, and a Stronghold Guard Commander names Stronghold Guards. By Skyrim's law only a Nord or an Imperial may sit as Jarl. Type /appoint on its own to list the zones.
/dismiss <player|#TAG> <zone>Remove someone from an office you could appoint them to, online or offline.
/tomesThe Synod tome shop (inside the Synod Conclave; college members, one tome a week).

Beast forms

Only if you carry the gift or the curse.

/beast [werewolf|vampirelord|revert]Take or leave your beast form (or cast the power).
/formsThe abilities and keys of the beast form you are in.
/bloodFor vampires: your rank among vampires, and whether the Blood Crown is held.
/huntFor werewolves: your rank in the Great Hunt, and your pack.

When things go badly

Down, gaoled, tied up, or something broken.

/respawnWhile down: stop waiting for help and wake at the temple.
/chillHow long the chill of the grave has left after waking at the temple.
/sentenceTime left on your sentence.
/struggleTry to break free when your hands are bound.
/bug <what went wrong>Report a bug. Where you are and what is around you are saved with it, so send it while the problem is in front of you.
/ticket <pk|mod|report> <what happened>A private ticket with staff on Discord: pk to ask for a player kill, mod for moderation help, report to report a player.

Reference

The systems behind them

Each of these has its own guide.

  • Starting out — what the alpha is, installing, and the reset at launch.
  • Skills — eighteen of them, one pool of 300 points. You can dabble in everything and master almost nothing.
  • Levels — what growing costs in hours, and what each level opens.
  • Crafting and trades — the forge, the loom, the fire and the other stations.
  • Magic — the schools of magic and how spells are learned.
  • Race — what your blood gives you, at no cost to your skill points.
  • Faith — one god at a time, prayed to at their shrine (or anywhere, for the older faiths) for a blessing.
  • Factions and holds — who you swear to, and who rules where.
  • Vampirism and lycanthropy — how you catch them, and what they cost.
  • The rules — nineteen rules of play and the standards every player agrees to.

Staff carry a further set of commands that are not listed here.
