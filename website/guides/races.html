Bloodlines of Tamriel

Ten peoples, ten inheritances. What your blood gives you here is fixed at birth, costs you nothing from the Wheel, and never has to be earned back.

The Baseline

Every bar is drawn against the Imperial line — 200 health, 200 magicka, 150 stamina — the centre line on each track. Left of it is a shortfall, right of it a gift.

No Cost to You

Blood is not a skill. None of this touches your 300 spokes of the Wheel — an Orc smith pays no more for the forge than anyone else.

Why these numbers

Every character is born with the same 400: 150 health, 150 magicka and 100 stamina. The blood adds its own 150 on top and decides where it goes. That is why the Imperial line sits at 200, 200 and 150, and why every figure on this page is the whole of what you start with, not a share of it.

The spread is borrowed from Imperious — Races of Skyrim, which is the most carefully balanced racial overhaul the community has produced. The resistances are not: Imperious halves vanilla's figures to pay for three scripted powers per race, and we are not taking those powers, so we do not pay that price. A Dunmer here resists fire at 50%, as they have since Morrowind.

Orc magic resistance is restored on purpose. The Orsimer carried it in Oblivion; Skyrim quietly dropped it and left them one shouted power and nothing else. It returns at 15% — enough to feel, not enough to take the crown from High Rock.

Khajiit and Argonian both bear claws, and the server has always counted them — but they were counted identically, which left the Khajiit with nothing of their own once you notice that seeing in the dark changes no number. Their claws now cut harder than any other hand in Tamriel. They are still given no resistance to anything, because they have never had one in any age, and a sheet that reads well matters less than one that reads true.

Every bloodline still sums to exactly 550. That is the rule the whole table is built on — points move between a race's own three stats and the total never changes, so no bloodline is simply better than another, only pointed somewhere else.
