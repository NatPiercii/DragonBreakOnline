<p align="center">Thorbald's Methods<br>EBONY</p>

<p align="left">From the notes of Thorbald the smith, as published by Adolphus Eritius in "Forge, Hammer and Anvil".</p>

<p align="left">"Ebony armor and weapons. Takes only Ebony. No iron. Not even a little. You'll want to, but don't."</p>

<p align="left">Every smith who reads that line wants to argue with it. Every smith who argued with it has ruined an ingot worth more than their anvil.</p>
