<p align="center">Thorbald's Methods<br>DWARVEN METAL</p>

<p align="left">From the notes of Thorbald the smith, as published by Adolphus Eritius in "Forge, Hammer and Anvil".</p>

<p align="left">"Dwarfen armor and weapons. Dwarfen scrap metal, some iron, and some leather strips. I'm gonna stop sayin leather, cawse it's always used."</p>

<p align="left">No living smith knows how the Dwemer made their metal. We only melt what they left behind and beat it into our own shapes. Take your scrap from the ruins, render it slowly, and do not ask it to be more than it was.</p>
