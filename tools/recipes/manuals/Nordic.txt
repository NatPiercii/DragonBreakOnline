<p align="center">CARVED NORDIC STEEL</p>

<p align="left">On Solstheim the Nords still carve their armour the way their grandfathers did, and the carving is not for show: the patterns follow the grain of the metal and make it stronger where a blow will land.</p>

<p align="left">Carved Nordic work is steel and quicksilver, cut before it is quenched. A smith used to plain plate will find it slow. A smith who masters it will not go back.</p>
