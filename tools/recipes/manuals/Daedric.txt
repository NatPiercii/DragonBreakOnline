<p align="center">WHAT THORBALD WOULD NOT WRITE</p>

<p align="left">When the old smith Thorbald dictated his methods (they are printed in "Forge, Hammer and Anvil"), he came to Daedric armor and said only: "Hah! Like I'd rite that down." His assistant added what he had seen: Thorbald used daedra hearts, and nobody knew where he got them.</p>

<p align="left">This much can be written. Daedric work is ebony first, and all of Thorbald's rules for ebony hold. The heart goes into the fire, not into the metal, and it is spent in the working. What the smith gives in return is not a matter for a book.</p>
