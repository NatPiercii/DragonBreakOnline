<p align="center">RINGS AND RIVETS</p>

<p align="left">The Legion's mail was the armour of Cyrodiil for a thousand years before plate came into fashion, and it is still what a sensible soldier wears on a long road.</p>

<p align="left">Draw your wire even, wind it on a rod, cut the coil into rings, and close every ring with a rivet. Four rings through one is the Colovian weave; the Nibenese use six and a lighter wire. It is slow work and dull work, and armour made in a hurry fails in a hurry.</p>
