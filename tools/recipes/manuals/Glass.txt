<p align="center">Thorbald's Methods<br>GLASS</p>

<p align="left">From the notes of Thorbald the smith, as published by Adolphus Eritius in "Forge, Hammer and Anvil".</p>

<p align="left">"Glass armor and weapon. Need Malachite and sum Moonstone. Nasty stuff, workin' with Malachite."</p>

<p align="left">Refined malachite is not glass as a glazier knows it. It will cut the hand that shapes it carelessly, and it will not forgive a cold forge.</p>
