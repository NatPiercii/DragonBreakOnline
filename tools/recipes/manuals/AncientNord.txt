<p align="center">BLADES OF THE OLD NORDS</p>

<p align="left">The weapons in the barrows were made when the Nords first came to Skyrim, and they have outlasted the hands that held them. They are not steel as we make it, but they are close to it, and a smith who studies them can make blades in the old manner.</p>

<p align="left">The old smiths honed their edges on stone and oiled them against the cold. The honed blades of the deepest barrows were given more care still, and are counted among the finest Nord work.</p>
