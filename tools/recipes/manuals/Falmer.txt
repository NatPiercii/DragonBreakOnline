<p align="center">WHAT THE DEEP ELVES FORGE</p>

<p align="left">No scholar has watched a Falmer smith at work and come back to write about it. What we know, we know from what they leave behind: blades and bows of chaurus chitin, and armour of the same shell, hardened in ways we can only copy.</p>

<p align="left">Take chitin from the chaurus, never from lesser beasts. Boil it, press it flat while it is soft, and let it cure in the dark. The Falmer do not use fire as we do, and a smith who tries to hurry the shell with heat will crack it.</p>
