<p align="center">Thorbald's Methods<br>ORCISH</p>

<p align="left">From the notes of Thorbald the smith, as published by Adolphus Eritius in "Forge, Hammer and Anvil".</p>

<p align="left">"Orcish armor and weapons. Use Orichalcum and a bit of iron." (His assistant adds: Thorbald doesn't like Orcs.)</p>

<p align="left">Thorbald's dislike did not stop him copying their work. The smiths of Orsinium hammer orichalcum thick and heavy and leave the hammer's mark on it with pride. Do the same.</p>
