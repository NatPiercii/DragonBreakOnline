<p align="center">SILVER OF THE REACH</p>

<p align="left">Markarth was built on silver, and the Silver-Bloods on Markarth. Most of the silver that leaves the Reach goes to jewellers, but a little goes to smiths, and those smiths are sought by anyone who hunts what walks at night.</p>

<p align="left">Silver alone is too soft to hold an edge. It is laid over steel, not forged instead of it. Keep the silver cool and the steel hot, and bring them together only once.</p>
