<p align="center">Thorbald's Methods<br>STEEL</p>

<p align="left">What follows is taken from the notes of Thorbald, a smith of ill temper who died before Tiber Septim's reign, as Adolphus Eritius published them in "Forge, Hammer and Anvil". Thorbald could not write; his assistant wrote for him, and did not always stop when told.</p>

<p align="left">"Steel armor and weapons. Lots of Steel ingots, a little iron, and some leather strips."</p>

<p align="left">That is the whole of it, in his words. Steel forgives a young smith less than iron does: heat it evenly, quench it once, and never twice. The Legion's plate, the Blades' old harness and the Dawnguard's are all steel under their different faces, and a smith who knows one knows the rest.</p>
