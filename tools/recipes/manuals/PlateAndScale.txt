<p align="center">Thorbald's Methods<br>PLATE AND SCALE</p>

<p align="left">From the notes of Thorbald the smith, as published by Adolphus Eritius in "Forge, Hammer and Anvil".</p>

<p align="left">"Steel armor and weapons. Lots of Steel ingots, a little iron, and some leather strips. Cept for Steel Plate. It needs some Corundum too."</p>

<p align="left">"Scaled armor too. Well, that needs some steel also. And Corundum. Can't make Scaled armor without Corundum. That would be dum."</p>

<p align="left">Plate and scale are steel taught patience. Beat the corundum into the steel until the colour changes, and not a stroke after.</p>
