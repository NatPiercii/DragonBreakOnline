<p align="center">THE ICE THAT WILL NOT MELT</p>

<p align="left">Stalhrim is ice from Solstheim that does not melt, and the Skaal say it holds the breath of the All-Maker. It cannot be mined by force: the Skaal teach that it comes away only for those who have learned the old way.</p>

<p align="left">Stalhrim is shaped cold. A forge fire will not soften it; it is carved and fitted, and bound to its frame with ebony. Those who have made it into armour say it keeps the cold even in summer, and that the cold bites whoever strikes it.</p>
