<p align="center">AMBER AND MADNESS</p>

<p align="left">The smiths of the Golden Saints and the Dark Seducers have left their craft behind in this world, and a few mortal smiths have learned it. Joften, who forged for the bandit companies that took the Daedra's names, wrote that the golden blades of the Saints are reinforced with refined moonstone, hammered while the gold is heated just below melting, and hardened by the heart of a Daedra in the forge. The dark armour of the Seducers needs more finesse, for it balances unyielding ebony with malleable quicksilver.</p>

<p align="left">Amber and madness ore are stranger still. The smith Evethra wrote that amber wants a delicate hand, and that madness may be bent and folded like sinew. Neither behaves as any metal of Tamriel does.</p>
