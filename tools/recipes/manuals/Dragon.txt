<p align="center">BONE AND SCALE</p>

<p align="left">A dragon's bones are harder than any metal of Tamriel, and its scales harder still to pierce. Few smiths have ever had either on their anvil, because few have lived to take them.</p>

<p align="left">Dragon bone is not melted. It is cut, shaped and bound, and it keeps the dragon's heat long after the dragon is dead. Scale is laid like armour tiles, each one overlapping the last, over a frame of leather and bone. It is the last work a master smith learns, and there is nothing after it.</p>
