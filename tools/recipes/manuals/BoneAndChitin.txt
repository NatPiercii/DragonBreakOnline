<p align="center">BONEMOLD AND CHITIN</p>

<p align="left">The Dunmer have never had iron to spare, so they armour themselves in what their land gives them. Tavi Dromio's old tale, "Bone", tells of the armorer Gorkith, who made the first bonemold for Arslic Oan's slaves; whether the tale is true or not, the craft is.</p>

<p align="left">Bonemold is bone shaped and set with resin, then fired until it is harder than the bone ever was. Chitin is the shell of beasts, cut, pressed and bound. Neither is worked like metal: the hammer is for fitting, not forging. A letter from one Glover Mallory of Raven Rock shows that even the best of these recipes are closely kept.</p>
