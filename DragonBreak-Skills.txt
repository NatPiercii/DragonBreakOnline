DRAGONBREAK ONLINE - SKILLS
Generated 2026-09-14 from server/skills.json

Rules
  Pick 3 skills per character (K key).
  Tiers: Novice (from start), Apprentice (10 hours), Journeyman (30 hours), Expert (70 hours), Master (150 hours)
  Hours are server-verified activity in that skill; one hour credited per hour of real work.
  Respec: standing stones (vanilla DoomStone activators: Warrior, Mage, Thief, Lover, Steed, Lady, Lord, Apprentice, Atronach, Ritual, Serpent, Shadow, Tower). First respec free, then 1200 gold. Dropped skills lose all progress.
  Vanilla level cap 5, perks disabled. Level = sum of tiers across the three chosen skills divided by 3, rounded down. Per level: +10 to one of Health, Magicka, Stamina chosen by the player.

Mini-games: mining = timing bar, one strike per hit inside the green zone, strikes per vein by Miner tier; woodcutting = rhythm press, strikes per sitting by Woodcutter tier (4-20); reading = word-order or timed page turn, result rolls the Scholar tables; lumberMill = no mini-game, 30-minute cooldown per player per mill, yield by Woodcutter tier

Praying: shrines of Akatosh, Arkay, Dibella, Julianos, Kynareth, Mara, Stendarr, Talos, Zenithar. Cooldown 60 min per shrine. Blessing chance 2% for anyone, Priest by tier 5% / 10% / 15% / 20% / 30%. Only at your own deity.
