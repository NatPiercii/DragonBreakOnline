# DragonBreak Online Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 1, 2026**

DragonBreak Online is a free, unofficial roleplay server for *The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim Special Edition*, operated by the DragonBreak Online team ("we," "us," or "our") from the United States. DragonBreak Online is for players **18 and over**.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and how you can request access or deletion.

## 1. What We Collect

We collect only information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and moderate DragonBreak Online.

### Discord account

When you log in through Discord, we may receive:

* Discord user ID
* Username
* Server roles and permissions

### Connection information

Our game server, launcher downloads, and website may receive or record:

* IP address
* Connection or access times
* Basic connection information needed to operate and secure the service

### Characters and game activity

The game server records information necessary to operate the persistent world, including:

* Character information and appearance
* Skills and progression
* Inventory and equipment
* Property and other game assets
* Chat
* Commands and actions
* Trades
* Combat
* Crafting
* Other gameplay activity necessary to operate or moderate the server

### Launcher diagnostics

The launcher records a short session report when a play session starts and ends. This may include:

* Whether the session ended normally or with a crash
* Launcher version
* Client version
* Game version

If you choose to submit a problem report, it may additionally include:

* Launcher and game logs, including the game's diagnostic log
* A crash log, when available
* Your description of the problem
* Memory/RAM
* Processor
* Graphics card
* Available disk space
* Windows version

Problem reports are only submitted when you choose to send them or agree to send one after a crash.

### Automatic error reports

When the game or the launcher runs into an error, it may send an automatic error report. This may include:

* The error message and where in our code it happened
* The game, client, and launcher versions, and your Windows version
* A short list of your most recent in-game actions, such as menus opened or items used
* Short excerpts of the game and launcher logs, with personal file paths removed
* Your DragonBreak account

Automatic error reports are used only to find and fix bugs. You can turn them off in the launcher where that option is available, and reports about a game crash ask for your agreement before they are sent.

### Bug reports and support

When you submit a `/bug` report, support ticket, or other report, we may receive the information you choose to provide. A `/bug` report may include a snapshot of your character or game state at the time of the report.

### Voice chat

DragonBreak Online uses a LiveKit voice server operated by us for proximity voice chat.

**We do not record or store game voice chat.** Voice is transmitted live between players participating in the service.

Other players may independently record gameplay or voice using their own software. We do not control those recordings.

### Website cookies

Our website sets a short-lived cookie while you sign in with Discord, and a session cookie while you are signed in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

### Information we do not receive

We do not receive or store your:

* Steam account credentials
* Nexus Mods account credentials
* Payment card information

## 2. Why We Collect Information

We use collected information to:

* Operate your account, characters, and the persistent game world.
* Maintain backups and restore game data when necessary.
* Connect players to the service and protect the service from abuse.
* Investigate cheating, exploits, harassment, and other rule violations.
* Enforce bans and other moderation decisions.
* Diagnose crashes and technical problems.
* Improve server stability and gameplay systems.
* Respond to support requests and bug reports.

We **do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or create advertising profiles about you**.

We aim to collect and retain only information reasonably necessary for these purposes. Privacy and security commitments made in this policy are intended to describe our actual practices accurately.

## 3. Third-Party Services

DragonBreak Online relies on third-party services that operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

These may include:

* **Discord** — authentication, community, communication, and support.
* **Steam** — your copy of *Skyrim Special Edition*.
* **Nexus Mods** — distribution of mods used by the service.
* **GitHub** — distribution of launcher and related project files.
* **LiveKit** — live proximity voice communication operated through infrastructure controlled by DragonBreak Online.
* **Cloudflare** — delivery and protection of our website and the launcher's connections to our servers. Cloudflare processes IP addresses and connection information when you use them.
* **Anthropic (Claude)** — AI assistants our staff use to help operate, troubleshoot, and moderate the service. They may process server logs, bug reports, and support tickets on our behalf.

We do not control how independent third-party services collect or process information under their own policies.

## 4. Who Can Access Your Information

Access to DragonBreak information is limited to DragonBreak staff who need it to:

* Operate the service.
* Provide support.
* Moderate players.
* Investigate reports.
* Diagnose and fix technical problems.
* Maintain the service and its security.

Bug reports or moderation information may be shared with appropriate staff or posted to our internal/Discord-based tracking systems when necessary to handle the report.

You may mark a launcher problem report as private when that option is available.

We do not share personal information outside the DragonBreak team except when necessary to operate the service through a service provider, when you ask us to do so, or when required by law.

## 5. How Long We Keep Information

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Our current target retention periods are:

| Information                          | Target retention |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------: |
| Account and active character data    | While your account remains active |
| World backups                        | 30 days |
| Server activity and connection logs  | 90 days |
| Launcher session and problem reports | 180 days |
| Bug-report snapshots                 | 180 days |
| Support and moderation records       | 2 years |
| Ban records                          | For the duration of the ban and as reasonably necessary to prevent ban evasion |
| Discord posts                        | According to Discord and server moderation practices |

Some information may be retained longer when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, enforce a ban, resolve a dispute, maintain security, preserve backups, or comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we intend to delete it or securely dispose of it.

## 6. Your Privacy Requests

You may contact us to request information about personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion of your account and personal information.

Requests can be made through:

* A DragonBreak Online Discord support ticket
* **[dragonbreakonline@gmail.com](mailto:dragonbreakonline@gmail.com)**

Deleting your account permanently removes your DragonBreak characters and ends your ability to use that account.

Some information may remain when we have a legitimate reason or legal obligation to retain it, including information necessary for security, fraud prevention, ban enforcement, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

If privacy laws applicable to you provide additional rights, we will handle requests according to those applicable requirements. California law, for example, provides certain rights concerning access, correction, and deletion of personal information, subject to exceptions.

## 7. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy as DragonBreak Online develops or our data practices change.

The current version will be published with its effective date. Significant changes may also be announced through the DragonBreak Online Discord.
